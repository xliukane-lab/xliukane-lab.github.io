Engineering design

Human-in-the-loop engineering design

How can a model propose alternatives grounded in engineering knowledge while leaving the designer room to compare and revise them?

Bringing engineering knowledge into concept design

A language model can suggest concepts from a brief, but each suggestion still needs to be considered against existing engineering knowledge. We study a conceptual design framework in which a knowledge graph supplies relationships between concepts, a language model helps generate alternatives, and designers take part in evaluation and revision.

Colour as a design constraint

Interface colour provides a concrete setting for studying how knowledge and examples can inform design. We combine colour design knowledge with patterns in existing schemes to generate alternatives for comparison. This raises a broader evaluation question: can a designer understand the basis of a suggestion well enough to make a meaningful change?
