Machine health

Machine health across operating conditions

A change in speed or load alters a machine’s signal. How can a diagnostic model distinguish that change from an actual fault?

Separating a fault from a change in load

A diagnostic model may be trained at one load and deployed at another. Because healthy signals also vary with operating conditions, a change in the signal can have more than one explanation. We investigate how to recognise rotating machinery faults when the conditions at test time differ from those observed during training.

Comparing evidence across modalities

Our work in Measurement extracts complementary information from multiple representations of machine condition and combines it for diagnosis. One question guides our reading of the results: which features remain informative about a fault when the operating conditions change?
