Research areas

Intelligent Manufacturing and Logistics ↗

Equipment failures disrupt production plans, while vehicle positions and incoming jobs change the choices available to a dispatcher. We study how operational data and machine condition can inform the next production or logistics decision.

Renewable Energy ↗

As a wind front crosses a farm, turbines encounter it at different times. We use that spatial relationship to estimate future winds and power output, then study how turbines can coordinate their operation under uncertain inflow.

Intelligent Design ↗

An engineering brief often starts with an incomplete description. We investigate how to recover functional requirements, generate concepts grounded in domain knowledge, and refine candidate designs through human judgement and efficient surrogate evaluation.

Research highlights

International Journal of Production Research · 2024

Dispatching AGVs as terminal jobs arrive ↗

Each dispatch changes both a vehicle’s position and the waiting time for later jobs. We formulated dispatch as a Markov decision process and trained a multi-agent policy to consider makespan and energy use together. Simulations compared operational and energy performance with benchmark policies across several yard sizes.

Energy · 2024

Generating alternative futures for a wind farm ↗

A single forecast curve cannot describe the different ways a wind front may travel through a farm. GAPGAN maps turbine histories to a graph and generates alternative wind trajectories from the same observations. Using records from 11 turbines, the study compared five benchmarks and examined trajectory shape as well as scenario diversity.

Advanced Engineering Informatics · 2026

Turning an ambiguous brief into a traceable concept ↗

KnowLoop structures requirements in terms of function, behaviour and structure. A domain knowledge graph and expert supervision then guide concept generation by a language model. Across three water-treatment equipment tasks, expert ratings averaged about 2.8% higher than FBStd over four evaluation criteria.
