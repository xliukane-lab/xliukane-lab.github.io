Renewable energy

Wind forecasting under uncertainty

When a turbine stops reporting, what can nearby machines tell us about the range of winds the farm may encounter in the next few hours?

From a turbine to a wind farm

As wind travels across a farm, measurements at one turbine carry information about conditions at its neighbours. We represent relationships between turbines as a graph and learn how wind speeds evolve over time. The model generates alternative short-term trajectories that preserve relationships across machines.

When observations go missing

Gaps in supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) records interrupt the history used by a forecaster. We combine simultaneous observations from nearby turbines with the past behaviour of the affected machine to reconstruct missing segments. In related work, we estimate a distribution for future power output so that a forecast also expresses its uncertainty.
