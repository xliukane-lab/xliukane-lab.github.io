Group lead · Master’s supervisor

Xin Liu

Xin Liu directs the Institute of Industrial and Intelligent Systems Engineering at Beijing Institute of Technology. He earned a bachelor’s degree in industrial engineering from BIT in 2016 and a PhD in data science from City University of Hong Kong in 2021.

His research uses operational data to characterise uncertainty in equipment and its environment, and studies how forecasts can inform control and scheduling. He has led Young Scientists Fund and General Program projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, with work on renewable energy scenarios and collaborative design.

Teaching and research training

Xin Liu teaches courses including Intelligent Manufacturing Systems and Sensing, Analysis and Decision-Making for Manufacturing Systems. Student research has examined how to forecast wind power from incomplete records and recognise faults in rotating machines across operating conditions.
