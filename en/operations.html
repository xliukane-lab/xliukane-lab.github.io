Industrial engineering

Scheduling and spatial optimisation

How should a terminal assign a vehicle to the next job, or a retailer allocate limited shelf space, when one choice restricts the next?

Choosing the next job at a terminal

Assigning an automated guided vehicle (AGV) to a container move changes which vehicles are available for later jobs and where they will be. We model dispatch as a sequence of decisions and study real-time policies using deep reinforcement learning, taking vehicle energy use into account alongside transport efficiency.

Allocating scarce space

On a two-dimensional shelf, moving one product changes the feasible placements of its neighbours. We combine a data-driven model with a hybrid genetic algorithm to guide the search over layouts. As in terminal dispatch, a candidate solution must respect the physical limits of the system.
